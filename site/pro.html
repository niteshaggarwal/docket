FAQ

Questions

Will the free version stay free?

Yes. Docket is open source under the AGPL-3.0, and everything you can self-host today stays free: the Windows, Android and web apps, the CLI, the AI connector, and every feature in them. New app features keep landing in the open-source version too.

When will Pro be available?

The phases ship in order, starting with Docket Cloud in Phase 1. There are no dates yet; we'll announce them, and the prices, as each phase gets close. Join the waitlist to hear first.

Can I self-host Pro features?

Features that need the hosted service, such as Docket Cloud itself, cloud backup, hosted AI planning credits, push notifications and calendar sync, only work with it. Features that are part of the apps land in the open-source apps, so you get them when you self-host too. The comparison above shows where a plan has a limit.

This plan may change. The roadmap shows what we intend to build and in what order, not a promise. Features, plans, limits and phases may move as we learn what people need. Nothing here is for sale yet, and pricing will be announced before anything is.
