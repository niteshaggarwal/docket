The apps

Each app connects to a Docket server: the Windows install sets one up on your PC, or use a Docker server.

Your device

Windows

Windows 10 or 11. The app, the task server, the docket command and the AI connector, all in one.

  1. Unzip the download into any folder.
  2. Right-click install.ps1 and choose Run with PowerShell.
  3. Answer a few questions, then open Docket from the Start menu.

Your device

Android

Android 8 or later. The same features as the Windows app, signed in to your own server.

  1. Open the APK. If Android asks, allow installing from your browser.
  2. Enter your server address, then your username and password.
  3. Using the Windows install? Reach your PC from the phone.
